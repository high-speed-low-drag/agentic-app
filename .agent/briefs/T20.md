## Problem
With T18's four-point voltage scale, within-band nodes on the results snapshot get the neutral `--c-volt-mid`. That's
`#f4f3ef` in light mode, so they look like empty circles with a thin ring. T12/T17's not-energised style
(`node[kind = "node"][energised = 0]`) is also a hollow circle with a thin muted ring, so the two are easy to confuse.

## Change
1. **Neutral fill:** light-mode `--c-volt-mid` becomes a clearly filled light grey, `#d6d5cf` (adjust slightly if needed
   for contrast), in `frontend/src/index.css` and the fallback in `components/charts/theme.ts`. Dark mode stays
   `#2a2a28`; check it still reads as a filled dot against the dark background with the fg2 ring, and adjust if it doesn't.
   Check that T18's colour mixing still starts from this neutral, and update the T18 unit tests if they hard-code
   the old value.
2. **Not-energised style** (`NetworkDiagram.tsx`), in both diagrams:
   - no fill (transparent background);
   - a dashed muted border (`border-style: dashed`, about 1.5 px);
   - a small "×" mark through the centre. Use an SVG background image from `symbols.ts` in the muted colour,
     regenerated on theme change like the other symbols;
   - the label in muted colour and italic (`font-style: italic`).
   It must have higher priority than the overlay voltage colour, which is null or NaN anyway for islanded nodes, and than
   the voltage-level colour in the case editor. The changed and selected cues must still layer on top as today.
3. **Legends:**
   - the status legend's "Not energised" entry shows the new dashed-ring-with-× icon;
   - the voltage scale legend's "within band" section shows the solid grey.
4. **Tooltips and details:** they already say "Not energised"; keep them.
